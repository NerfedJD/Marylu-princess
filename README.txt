FILES
-----
index.html  - Website structure
style.css   - Pink romantic design
script.js   - Questions, navigation, and Formspree submission

HOW TO USE IN ACODE
-------------------
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. In Acode, open the extracted date-project folder.
3. Open index.html and use Preview.
4. Test the whole flow and submit a test response while connected to the internet.
5. Check your Formspree dashboard/email to confirm the submission arrived.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Formspree may require you to verify your email or confirm the form setup before submissions work.
- Do not share the site until you have tested that answers arrive.
- The Formspree endpoint is visible in the website's JavaScript. That is normal for a browser form; do not use it for secret/private data.
- If CSS/JavaScript do not load, keep index.html, style.css, and script.js together in the same folder.
